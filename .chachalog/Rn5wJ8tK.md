An item moved in an orderable list, a multiple field or a multiple picker now keeps the focus and is outlined where it has landed, whether it was moved with the first, up, down and last buttons or by dragging it. Those buttons also stay reachable on the moved item, where before they were hidden again as soon as the item travelled out from under the pointer.
