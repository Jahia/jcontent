The menu on an image reference field in Content Editor now offers "Show image metadata", opening the
EXIF and XMP/IPTC read out of that image with a copy button after each value. Copying closes the
dialog in the same click, leaving the editor back on the form ready to paste — so a caption, a credit
or a copyright notice can be taken from the image without going to look it up in the media manager.
The entry only appears on an image that carries such metadata.
