# Allowed version bumps: patch, minor, major
"@jahia/jcontent": minor
---

The preview of a page or a content now has a width selector (1280, 1024, 768 or 375 px, opened on 768 px). The content is rendered at that width and scaled down to fit the pane, so a responsive layout no longer collapses to its narrow form in a narrow pane. The selector is in the preview of the side panel and of the Content Editor. It is not shown for files and folders.
